good questions

Things people usually ask

Everything you need to know before your very first campaign.

Do I need to create an account?
No account, no registration, no email verification. Open the app, connect your email (Google OAuth or SMTP) and start sending immediately.
Is my contact list safe? Where is it stored?
Your CSV/Excel file is parsed entirely in your browser's memory with JavaScript. It's never uploaded to any server. Close the tab or refresh and it's gone — nothing is persisted on a backend database.
What is Google OAuth, and why is it better?
Google OAuth authorizes the app through Google's official login screen — no separate App Password needed. The app receives a short-lived token stored only in your browser session. When you close the browser, it's gone. For manual SMTP mode you'll still need an App Password from your Google security settings.
How do I send different files to each person?
In Step 2 (Recipients), upload your attachments into the "Load Attachments" dropzone (they load into browser memory). In your CSV, add a column (e.g. "Attachment") with the exact filename for each row. Then map that column in the Attachment Path field. Each person receives only their own file.
How do I keep emails out of spam?
Set a delay of 3–5 seconds between emails in Step 4 to avoid spam velocity filters. Use a verified sender domain with SPF and DKIM records. And personalize with names like {Name} — generic mass mail is far more likely to get flagged.
Can I pause or stop a campaign mid-way?
Yes. During sending, Pause and Stop stay active. Pause lets you resume right where you left off. Stop ends the campaign and shows the final stats. The live log shows exactly which emails were sent and which failed.
