Google OAuth — Send Without an App Password

Connect your Gmail account securely via Google's official OAuth 2.0 flow. No need to enable "less secure apps" or generate App Passwords. Click Connect, authorize once, and you're ready to send using the Gmail API directly. Your token is stored only in your browser session.

Any SMTP Provider

Prefer Outlook, Yahoo, or a private SMTP relay? Switch to Manual SMTP mode. Enter your server, port, encryption (TLS/SSL) and App Password. One-click presets for Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, and Office365.

Excel & CSV Import + In-App Editing

Drop any .xlsx, .xls or .csv file. The app parses it instantly in your browser. Made a typo? Click any cell in the preview table to edit it on the spot. Add new rows or columns without reopening Excel.

Dynamic Variable Personalization

Any column from your spreadsheet becomes an insertable variable. Click a chip to insert {Name}, {Company}, or {CRN} anywhere in the subject or body.

Per-Recipient File Attachments

Send different attachments to each recipient. Upload your files directly into the app (stored in browser memory, never uploaded to a server). Map an attachment column in your CSV — each person gets their own file like a personalised invoice, certificate, or report. Supports any file type. FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before sending your first campaign.

Do I need to create an account?
No account, no registration, no email verification. Open the app, connect your email (Google OAuth or SMTP), and start sending immediately.
Is my contact list safe? Where is it stored?
Your CSV/Excel file is parsed entirely in your browser's memory using JavaScript. It is never uploaded to any server. Once you close the tab or refresh, it's gone — nothing is persisted on a backend database.
What is Google OAuth and why is it better than an App Password?
Google OAuth lets you authorize the app through Google's official login screen without creating a separate App Password. The app receives a short-lived access token stored only in your browser session. When you close the browser, the token is gone. For SMTP mode, you still need an App Password from your Google account security settings.
How do I send different attachments to each recipient?
In Step 2 (Recipients), upload your attachment files into the "Load Attachments" dropzone (they load into browser memory). In your CSV, add a column (e.g. "Attachment") with the exact filename for each row. Then map that column in the Attachment Path field. Each person will receive only their own file.
How do I stop emails from going to spam?
Set a delay of 3–5 seconds between emails in Step 4 to avoid triggering spam velocity filters. Use a verified sender domain with SPF and DKIM records configured. Personalize your emails with recipient names using variables like {Name} — generic mass emails are more likely to be flagged.
Can I pause or stop a campaign mid-way?
Yes. During sending, the Pause and Stop buttons are active. Pause lets you resume from where you left off. Stop ends the campaign and shows the final stats. The live log shows which emails were sent and which failed.
